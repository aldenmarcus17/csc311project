\paragraph{Decision-tree results.}
The tree correctly classified 746 of 870 outer-fold responses
(85.75\% accuracy, macro-F1 0.857).
Accuracy ranged from 83.33\% to 87.36\%
across the five folds. Mean outer training accuracy was 96.67\%,
a gap of 10.92 percentage points.
Holding the minimum leaf size at one, mean inner validation accuracy across the five
searches was 77.84\% at depth 3, 84.65\% at
depth 10 and 83.97\% at unlimited depth.
The search used 300 initial inner fits, 0 expansion
fits and 5 outer refits.

Precision for Baseball, Basketball and Soccer was 83.22\%, 88.05\%, 85.96\%, respectively.
Confusion between Baseball and Soccer accounted for 57 of 124 errors:
28 Baseball responses were classified as Soccer, and 29 went
the other way. Preliminary inspection of responses informed the shared keyword dictionary,
so these development scores may be optimistic for new respondents.
